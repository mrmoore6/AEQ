DISTORTION PREVENTION MEASURES
=====================================================

1. SOFT-KNEE LIMITING:
   - Threshold: 1.2 (allows more headroom)
   - Knee Width: 0.8 (smooth transition)
   - Cubic curve algorithm for musical limiting
   - Safety margins: 5% above knee, 2% within knee

2. DYNAMIC RANGE:
   - Overall range: ±1.8dB (optimized for clarity)
   - Allows sufficient boost for 3D effect
   - Prevents excessive gain that causes distortion

3. PEAK LIMITING (Headroom Management):
   - Peak > 95%: -1.8dB (strong limiting)
   - Peak > 88%: -1.0dB (moderate ceiling)
   - Peak > 78%: -0.5dB (soft ceiling)
   - Peak > 68%: -0.25dB (gentle management)
   - Normal levels: 0.0dB (transparent for maximum clarity)

4. ADAPTIVE SMOOTHING:
   - Faster response for cuts (reduce distortion)
   - Slower response for boosts (prevent pumping)
   - Smoothing factor: 0.28 for cuts, 0.10 for boosts
